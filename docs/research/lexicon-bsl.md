# BSL (British Sign Language) starter lexicon

Research date: 2026-10-31. Signer's perspective, dominant hand = right (H1), non-dominant = left (H2).

## Sources

| Key | Source | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| BS | https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/<word>/ | Short **textual** production description per sign (quoted below in "Source text"). The only text source found. |
| SB | https://www.signbsl.com/sign/<word> | Video only (Bristol/Deaf Studies Trust, NDCS, Deafway etc.), several variants per word. No text. |

## Confidence rules

- **high**: core form quoted verbatim from BS; orientation details I added are standard for that form and flagged "(inferred)".
- **med**: not in BS text; form is well known in BSL teaching material, but I could not read it from a text source (videos not viewable by me). Check against SB/BSB video before animating.
- **low**: form from memory with known regional variation; must be checked against video.

## Regional variation (important)

BSL has strong regional lexical variation, mainly in numbers, colours, days/time words and some common verbs (FINISH has 8 variants in BSB). For an avatar, pick one variant per sign (the BS/Signature Level 1 form below, roughly a "southern / London" standard) and keep it consistent. Time words (TODAY, TOMORROW) and WEATHER are the most variable entries here.

BSL uses a **two-handed fingerspelling alphabet**, so initialised signs (NAME/DEAF "N", FAMILY "F") use two-handed letters, not ASL one-handed letters. "N" shape in BS text = index + middle extended together (H/U shape), laid on the other palm when spelling, used alone in NAME/DEAF.

## Lexicon

### HELLO (also HI, GOODBYE in BS)
- Source text: "Wave hand near side of head." (BS)
- Hands: one (H1).
- Handshape: B-open / 5 (flat hand, fingers together or slightly spread).
- Location: beside the head at temple/ear height, slightly forward (inferred).
- Orientation: palm forward to viewer, fingers up (inferred).
- Movement: side-to-side wave, 2-3 small oscillations from the wrist; a single outward movement from the temple (salute-like) is also common in video (SB).
- Non-manual: smile, eye contact; mouthing "hello".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/hello/ , https://www.signbsl.com/sign/hello
- Note: BS also lists GOOD (A-open forward) as "hi/hello" (thumbs-up greeting).

### GOODBYE
- Source text: "Wave hand near side of head." (BS) - same sign as HELLO.
- Hands: one. Handshape: B-open/5. Location: side of head. Palm forward, fingers up (inferred).
- Movement: wave side to side, or fingers flex down repeatedly (bye-bye wave) (inferred, common variant).
- Non-manual: mouthing "bye"/"goodbye".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/goodbye/

### THANK-YOU (also THANKS, PLEASE)
- Source text: "Flat hand starts with fingertips on chin. Hand moves down and away from signer." (BS)
- Hands: one (two-handed version exists for emphasis, both hands mirrored).
- Handshape: B (flat, fingers together, thumb alongside).
- Location: fingertips touching the chin / just below the lower lip.
- Orientation: palm back toward signer (and slightly up), fingers up at start; end palm up, fingers forward (inferred).
- Movement: single movement down and forward away from chin, ending in neutral space at chest height.
- Non-manual: slight head nod, smile; mouthing "thank you".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/thank-you/

### PLEASE
- Source text: identical to THANK-YOU (BS lists please/thanks/thank-you as one sign).
- Same parameters as THANK-YOU; mouthing "please" distinguishes it.
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/please/

### SORRY
- Source text: "Closed hand makes a circular motion on chest." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: S (closed fist, thumb across fingers) - "closed hand" in BS = fist.
- Location: centre of chest (sternum).
- Orientation: palm back to signer / knuckles forward-in (inferred).
- Movement: circular rubbing on the chest, typically 2 circles (clockwise from signer's view) (inferred repetition).
- Non-manual: apologetic face (brows slightly raised and drawn together, lips pressed/downturned); mouthing "sorry".
- Confidence: high (core), med (circle direction/count). URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/sorry/

### YES
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: head nod is the primary YES; manual sign = H1 closed fist (S/A) at chest height, palm down/forward, wrist nods down once or twice (fist "nodding"), accompanied by head nod.
- Non-manual: head nod is essential; mouthing "yes".
- Confidence: med. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/yes

### NO
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: H1 B-open/flat hand in neutral space, palm forward-down, makes a short sideways sweep outward (to right); variant: both flat hands, palms down, crossed at wrists, sweep apart sideways. Index finger wagging side-to-side is also used.
- Non-manual: head shake is essential; mouthing "no".
- Confidence: low (several variants). URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/no

### I / ME
- Source text: "Index finger points at signer." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: 1 (index extended).
- Location: in front of / touching centre of chest.
- Orientation: fingertip pointing back at signer (in), palm in/to the side.
- Movement: short movement toward chest, may touch once.
- Non-manual: none required; mouthing "me".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/me/

### YOU
- Source text: "Point with index finger at the person being referred to." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: 1.
- Location: neutral space at chest height.
- Orientation: fingertip forward toward addressee, palm down or to the side.
- Movement: short forward point (directional: aim at the person; for the viewer, straight forward).
- Non-manual: eye gaze at addressee.
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/you/

### NAME
- Source text: "Tips of index and middle fingers ('N' shape) touch forehead. Hand twists forwards at wrist until fingers are facing forwards in front of face." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: U/H (index + middle extended together, others closed).
- Location: fingertips on the forehead (at the side/front).
- Orientation: start palm toward the signer / fingers up-in touching forehead; end palm down-in, fingers pointing forward in front of face.
- Movement: wrist twist forward away from forehead, single.
- Non-manual: mouthing "name"; for "what's your name" add brow lowering (wh-question).
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/name/

### GOOD
- Source text: "Make short forward movement using closed hand with thumb up (1 or 2 hands)." (BS)
- Hands: one (or two, symmetric, for emphasis).
- Handshape: A-open (fist, thumb up).
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Orientation: palm in to midline (left), thumb up, knuckles forward.
- Movement: short single forward movement (repeat/stronger for "great").
- Non-manual: positive face, slight nod; mouthing "good".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/good/

### BAD
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form (antonym of GOOD): H1 I handshape (little finger extended, fist, thumb closed), palm in to midline, little finger up, short forward movement in neutral space (one or two hands).
- Non-manual: negative face (nose wrinkle, lips down); mouthing "bad".
- Confidence: med (this pinkie BAD is a standard BSL contrast with thumb GOOD). URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/bad

### HELP
- Source text: "Closed hand with thumb up rests on palm of flat upturned hand." (BS)
- Hands: two; H2 is a base.
- Handshapes: H1 A-open (thumbs up); H2 B (flat).
- Location: neutral space in front of chest/stomach.
- Orientation: H2 palm up, fingers forward/right; H1 palm in to midline, thumb up, little-finger edge resting on H2 palm.
- Movement: both hands move forward together (directional verb: toward the person helped) (inferred from video practice; BS text only gives the hold).
- Non-manual: mouthing "help".
- Confidence: high (hold), med (movement). URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/help/

### LOVE
- Source text: "Flat hands cross over on left side of chest." (BS)
- Hands: two, crossed.
- Handshape: B (flat) both.
- Location: left side of chest (heart).
- Orientation: palms back on chest, fingers pointing up-and-out diagonally, wrists crossed (right hand over left) (inferred).
- Movement: hands move in to the chest and hold (crossed hug); may press once.
- Non-manual: warm expression; mouthing "love".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/love/

### FRIEND
- Source text: "Dominant hand clasps non-dominant hand. Hands make a small up and down movement in front of body." (BS)
- Hands: two, clasped (handshake-like).
- Handshape: both B-bent/C-like grasping; H1 wraps around H2.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Orientation: H2 palm in, fingers right; H1 grasps from above, palm down-in (inferred).
- Movement: small up-down shaking, 2-3 times.
- Non-manual: mouthing "friend".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/friend/
- Variant: thumbs-up hands clasped together is also seen (SB videos).

### FAMILY
- Source text: "Fingerspelled 'F' makes small clockwise circles in front of body." (BS)
- Hands: two. BSL fingerspelled F = index + middle of H1 (U/H shape) laid across the extended index + middle of H2, both pointing roughly in toward the midline, palms down/back.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Movement: the joined F-hands make small horizontal clockwise circles (seen from above), 2 times.
- Non-manual: mouthing "family".
- Confidence: high (text), med (exact F orientation). URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/family/

### DEAF
- Source text: "Index and middle fingers extended ('N' shape) touch ear." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: U/H (index + middle extended together).
- Location: ear (fingertips touch the ear/ear canal area, right side).
- Orientation: palm toward signer / fingers point up-back to ear (inferred).
- Movement: touch ear (single contact; often a double tap).
- Non-manual: mouthing "deaf".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/deaf/

### HEARING (hearing person)
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: H1 index finger (1) points to/touches ear, then touches mouth (ear-mouth = hearing/speaking person). Some regions use just index finger tapping the ear or a cupped hand at ear for the verb "hear".
- Non-manual: mouthing "hearing".
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/hearing

### SIGN-LANGUAGE
- Source text: "Both open hands with palms facing each other make forward circular movements in front of body." (BS)
- Hands: two, symmetric, alternating.
- Handshape: B-open / 5 (open, relaxed).
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Orientation: palms facing each other (in to midline), fingers forward.
- Movement: alternating forward circles (vertical plane; like rolling forward), 2-3 cycles.
- Non-manual: mouthing "sign" / "sign language".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/sign-language/

### WATER
- Source text: "Flat hand makes short downwards movement on throat twice." (BS; same sign = THIRSTY)
- Hands: one. Handshape: B (flat).
- Location: throat / neck front.
- Orientation: palm down or in, fingers left (inferred), edge/fingertips on throat.
- Movement: short downward stroke on the throat, twice.
- Non-manual: mouthing "water".
- Confidence: high (text), but note other common variants (index finger brushing chin; "W" hand at mouth). URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/water/

### EAT
- Source text: "Bunched fingers and thumb of dominant hand tap mouth twice." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: O-flat (fingertips bunched to thumb).
- Location: mouth.
- Orientation: fingertips toward the mouth (back), palm back/down.
- Movement: tap to the mouth twice.
- Non-manual: mouthing "eat" / chewing mouth movement.
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/eat/

### DRINK
- Source text: "Dominant hand mimes drinking out of a glass." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: C (holding a glass).
- Location: starts just in front of chin, tilts to mouth.
- Orientation: palm in to midline, thumb side up/toward mouth.
- Movement: hand rises to the mouth and tilts back (wrist rotation), once or twice.
- Non-manual: mouthing "drink".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/drink/

### HOME (= HOUSE)
- Source text (under HOUSE, listed as also HOME): "Both flat hands held at an angle touch at tips to form the shape of a roof." (BS)
- Hands: two, mirrored.
- Handshape: B both.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest / face height.
- Orientation: palms facing down-in (toward each other at an angle), fingertips touching at the top (roof apex).
- Movement: hands touch at tips, then move apart and down outlining roof and walls (common in video; BS text only gives the roof hold).
- Non-manual: mouthing "home".
- Confidence: high (handshape/hold), med (outline movement). URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/house/

### WORK (also JOB, CAREER)
- Source text: "Both flat hands at right angles - one above the other. Bottom edge of dominant hand taps top edge of the non-dominant hand." (BS)
- Hands: two; H2 is a base.
- Handshape: B both.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Orientation: H2 palm in to midline (right), fingers forward; H1 palm in to midline (left), fingers forward, crossing at right angles above (or H1 palm left, fingers left). Contact: little-finger edge of H1 on index edge of H2.
- Movement: H1 chops down onto H2, tap twice.
- Non-manual: mouthing "work".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/work/

### TODAY
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form = NOW (+ DAY): both hands Y or B-open, palms up, short simultaneous downward drop in front of body. Highly regional; many signers sign NOW alone for "today".
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/today

### TOMORROW
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: H1 index finger (1) at the side of the chin/cheek, palm back, moves forward in an arc (future = forward). Regional variants exist.
- Non-manual: mouthing "tomorrow".
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/tomorrow

### WHAT
- Source text: "Extended index finger of dominant hand held in front of body. Hand makes repeated short side-to-side movements. With questioning expression." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: 1.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest.
- Orientation: palm forward or in, index pointing up/forward (inferred).
- Movement: repeated short side-to-side shakes.
- Non-manual: wh-question face (brows lowered/furrowed, head slightly forward); mouthing "what".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/what/

### WHERE
- Source text: "Both flat hands with palms facing upwards make circular movements in front of body - with questioning expression." (BS)
- Hands: two, symmetric (mirrored circles).
- Handshape: B / B-open.
- Location: neutral space in front of chest/stomach.
- Orientation: palms up, fingers forward.
- Movement: small horizontal circles (outward), 2 times; variant: alternating up-down.
- Non-manual: wh-question face (brows down); mouthing "where".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/where/

### WHO
- Source text: "Extended index finger on dominant hand held vertically to side of head and makes small circular movements." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: 1.
- Location: beside the head / near chin-cheek (BS: side of head).
- Orientation: index pointing up, palm forward or in (inferred).
- Movement: small circles, repeated.
- Non-manual: wh-question face; mouthing "who".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/who/

### HOW
- Not in BS text dictionary (only phrases HOW-ARE-YOU, HOW-OLD).
- Common BSL form: both hands B-bent (fingers bent at knuckles), backs of the fingers in contact, knuckles pointing down/in; hands rotate forward/up around each other so palms turn up. Wh-question face.
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/how

### WHY
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: H1 index finger (1) or flat hand taps the side of the forehead/temple, then may move forward; question face. Several variants.
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/why

### WANT (also NEED)
- Source text: "Flat dominant hand moves down chest then twists out." (BS)
- Hands: one. Handshape: B.
- Location: starts on upper chest, moves to lower chest/stomach.
- Orientation: palm back against chest, fingers in (left); at end twists so palm faces down/forward.
- Movement: brush down the chest, then wrist twists outward (away from body).
- Non-manual: mouthing "want".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/want/

### KNOW
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Common BSL form: H1 A-open (thumb extended), thumb tip taps the side of the forehead/temple, palm left/in; single or double tap. Mouthing "know".
- Confidence: med (thumb-on-forehead KNOW is the standard BSL citation form). URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/know

### UNDERSTAND
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Not verifiable from text. Reported forms include index finger at the forehead moving/flicking up and out. Do not animate without checking video.
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/understand

### MORE
- Not in BS text dictionary.
- Not verifiable from text. One reported form: H1 flat hand brushes up off the back of H2 / H1 hand moves up from H2 stacked hands. Check video.
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/more

### FINISH / DONE
- Not in BS text dictionary. BSB lists **8 variants** (keywords: been, complete, did, done, finished).
- Commonly taught form: both hands A-open (thumbs up), palms in, start in front of chest and move sharply forward-down / thumbs flick outward. Another common form: both B hands palms down, move apart sideways.
- Confidence: low (strong regional variation). URL: https://bslsignbank.ucl.ac.uk/dictionary/words/finish-1.html

### WEATHER
- Not in BS text dictionary. Not verifiable. Omitted from animation until checked (variants based on 5-hands moving above head).
- Confidence: low. URL (video): https://www.signbsl.com/sign/weather

### RAIN
- Source text: "Both open hands with palms facing downwards make short repeated downward movements in front of body. Dominant hand remains slightly higher than non-dominant hand." (BS)
- Hands: two, parallel (same direction, not mirrored), H1 slightly higher.
- Handshape: 5 / B-open (fingers spread, slightly curved).
- Location: neutral space at shoulder-to-chest height.
- Orientation: palms down, fingers forward (slightly down).
- Movement: short repeated downward movements, 2-3 times (fingers may flutter).
- Non-manual: mouthing "rain".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/rain/

### SUN
- Source text: "Closed hand starts at side and in front of head - hand opens while making a short inward movement." (BS)
- Hands: one.
- Handshape: start O-flat / S (closed), end 5 (open, spread) - rays.
- Location: above and to the right side, in front of the head.
- Orientation: fingertips toward the signer's face / palm down-back (rays shine onto signer).
- Movement: short inward/downward movement toward the face while opening (handshape change S/O-flat -> 5), once (twice for "sunny").
- Non-manual: slightly squinted eyes; mouthing "sun".
- Confidence: high. URL: https://www.british-sign.co.uk/british-sign-language/how-to-sign/sun/
