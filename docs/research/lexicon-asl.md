# ASL (American Sign Language): starter lexicon

Research notes for the 3D signing avatar. Signer's perspective, dominant hand = right (R), non-dominant = left (L).

## Sources

- Lifeprint / ASL University (Bill Vicars): https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/ (pages fetched 2026-10-31, URL per sign below)
- HandSpeak (Jolanta Lapiak): https://www.handspeak.com/word/ (pages fetched 2026-10-31, URL per sign below)

## How to read this file

- Text in "quotes" is quoted from the source. Everything else (exact palm direction, finger direction, end position) is standard citation form, filled in where the source text is short and the source video shows it. These additions are why some rows are marked **med**.
- Confidence: **high** = source text gives handshape, location and movement, and they agree with standard citation form; **med** = source text is partial, details added from the standard form or video; **low** = could not verify reliably.
- Handshape names follow the requested list. ASL "10" = A-open (fist, thumb up). "Flat O" = O-flat.
- ASL has regional and generational variants. Only the most common citation form is listed, with the main variant in the notes.

## Lexicon

### HELLO
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: B (flat hand, thumb alongside or slightly out).
- Start: side of the forehead / temple, like a salute; fingers point up, palm forward/out.
- Movement: "moves away from the headside", outward and slightly forward, in a small arc to the right. Single movement.
- Non-manual: "a pleasant expression", smile.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/1015/ ; Lifeprint mentions an informal wave as an alternative: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/hello.htm

### GOODBYE (BYE)
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: B-open, held up at about shoulder height in neutral space, palm forward, fingers up.
- Movement: "Wave goodbye with a bending of the large knuckles or at the wrist." Fingers fold down together at the knuckles and open again (B to B-bent and back), 2-3 times. Not side-to-side (that is the HI wave).
- Non-manual: friendly expression.
- Confidence: **med** (source text is short; the rest is the common form). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/g/goodbye.htm

### THANK-YOU
- Hands: one (R); two-handed form = "thank you very much".
- Handshape: B (flat hand).
- Start: fingertips touch the chin / lips, palm toward the signer (in), fingers up.
- Movement: "Move your hand forward and a bit down in the direction of the person you are thanking." The palm turns up as the hand moves out. Single movement.
- Non-manual: smile; can mouth "thank you".
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/t/thankyou.htm ; https://www.handspeak.com/word/2186/

### PLEASE
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: B (flat hand).
- Location: palm flat on the center of the chest, palm in (touching), fingers point left/up.
- Movement: "a circular motion ... a few times", circles on the chest, clockwise as the viewer sees it. 2-3 circles, the hand stays in contact.
- Non-manual: polite or pleading expression.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/p/please.htm

### SORRY
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: A (fist, thumb against the side of the index). Some signers use S.
- Location: center of the chest, palm in (thumb side up).
- Movement: "Rotate your hand on your chest using a couple of clockwise motions." Same circle as PLEASE, but with a fist.
- Non-manual: "a 'sorry' facial expression" (brows up and together, lips pressed together or a slight frown).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/s/sorry.htm

### YES
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: S (fist).
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest/shoulder, palm forward/down.
- Movement: "bobbing down twice at wrist, resembling a head nodding." Bend at the wrist only, twice.
- Non-manual: head nod at the same time (optional but common).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/2443/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/y/yes.htm

### NO
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: starts with index and middle fingers extended together and the thumb out (open H/U plus thumb). They close onto the thumb pad.
- Location: neutral space at shoulder height, "palm facing outward" (forward/down).
- Movement: index and middle fingers "tap with the thumb twice" (snap closed, open, closed). A single firm snap = emphatic NO.
- Non-manual: head shake, frown or brows down.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/1496/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/n/no.htm

### I / ME
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: 1 (index finger).
- Location: center of the chest; fingertip points in to the signer, palm left/down.
- Movement: "point at yourself or touch your index finger to your chest". Single touch.
- Non-manual: none.
- Note: the pronoun "I" can also be an I handshape on the chest (initialized, used in signed English). Native ASL uses the index point.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/m/me.htm

### YOU
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: 1.
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest; index points forward at the addressee (toward the viewer), palm down.
- Movement: short point forward, single.
- Non-manual: eye gaze at the addressee.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/2448/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/y/you.htm

### NAME
- Hands: two. L is the base, R moves.
- Handshape: H on both hands (index and middle fingers extended together, horizontal).
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest. Both H hands are held horizontal, fingers point forward/slightly in, palms in (edge down, thumb side up), crossed diagonally to form an X.
- Movement: the middle-finger edge of R "taps twice on the top edge of the index finger" of L. Double tap = noun; single tap = verb "named/called".
- Non-manual: neutral; in "NAME YOU?" use the wh-question face (brows down, head slightly forward).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/1464/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/n/name.htm

### GOOD
- Hands: one (R), or two (L palm-up base).
- Handshape: B (flat).
- Start: "fingers of your right hand against your lips", palm in, fingers up.
- Movement: moves forward/down; the palm turns up as the hand drops. In the two-handed form, R lands on L's upturned palm (back of R in L's palm, both palms up).
- Non-manual: neutral or pleased; may mouth "good".
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/g/good.htm

### BAD
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: B (flat).
- Start: fingertips on the lips, palm in.
- Movement: "Move your hand down and away. End with your palm facing downward." The hand flips over as it drops.
- Non-manual: negative face (frown, nose wrinkle).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/b/bad.htm

### HELP
- Hands: two. L is the moving base, R is on top.
- Handshape: R = A-open ("10", thumb up) or loose A/S, resting with the pinky side on L's palm. L = B-open, palm up, fingers forward.
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest/stomach.
- Movement: L lifts R straight up a few inches; both move together. Directional: moved toward the addressee = "I help you", toward the signer = "you help me".
- Non-manual: none required.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/help.htm

### LOVE
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: S (fists). A variant uses B (flat hands).
- Location: wrists crossed over the center of the chest, both palms in (toward the body), like hugging.
- Movement: hands come in and press onto the chest crossed, single hold.
- Non-manual: warm expression.
- Confidence: **med** (Lifeprint text gives only "crossing both hands over the middle of your chest"; S-handshape and wrist crossing are the standard citation form). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/l/love.htm

### FRIEND
- Hands: two.
- Handshape: X (index fingers hooked) on both hands.
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest.
- Movement: "interlocks the index fingers twice". R hooked index (palm down) hooks over L hooked index (palm up). Then the hands flip so L is on top and they hook again.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **med** (the flip sequence is from the standard form and video; the source text is short). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/f/friend.htm

### FAMILY
- Hands: two, mirrored.
- Handshape: F (index and thumb touch in a circle, other three fingers extended) on both hands.
- Start: neutral space in front of the chest. The F-hands touch at the index/thumb circles, palms forward, fingers up.
- Movement: "trace the shape of a circle". The hands move apart and around horizontally, out to the sides and forward, and meet again with the pinky sides touching, palms in toward the signer.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/f/family.htm

### DEAF
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: 1.
- Start: index fingertip on the cheek near the ear. "the right palm can face either left or somewhat forward."
- Movement: "Small arc" to the cheek near the mouth, touching. The order ear-to-mouth or mouth-to-ear is free in everyday use.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/d/deaf.htm

### HEARING (person who hears)
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: 1, held horizontal, index points left (in to the midline), palm down.
- Location: in front of the lips/chin.
- Movement: "moves forward in small circular motion a few times" (rolls up, forward, down, back, about twice).
- Non-manual: none.
- Note: the ability to hear is shown by tapping the ear twice; this is a different sign.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/1001/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/hearing.htm

### SIGN-LANGUAGE (compound SIGN + LANGUAGE)
- Part 1, SIGN: two hands, 1-handshape, palms facing each other/forward, index fingers pointing up and in. They make alternating circles toward the body ("pedaling" motion), "one hand half a circle ahead of the other".
- Part 2, LANGUAGE: two F hands (or L hands, initialized), thumbs/index tips touching or close together in front of the chest, palms forward/down. "Pull them apart" to the sides with "a slight twisting/shaking movement".
- Non-manual: none.
- Note: the everyday form is often SIGN alone. Bare "ASL" is fingerspelled A-S-L.
- Confidence: **med** (both parts verified separately; the compound is the standard citation form). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/s/sign.htm ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/l/language.htm

### WATER
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: W (index, middle and ring fingers extended and spread; thumb holds the pinky).
- Location: chin/mouth; palm left (in to the midline), fingers up.
- Movement: "Touch the index finger to your mouth twice" (taps the chin/lower lip).
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/water.htm

### EAT (FOOD)
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: O-flat ("squished O"; fingertips gathered on the thumb).
- Location: just in front of the mouth; palm in/down, fingertips toward the lips.
- Movement: small move to the lips "as if stuffing a piece of food into your mouth". Single = EAT; double tap = FOOD.
- Non-manual: "Keep your mouth closed".
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/e/eat.htm

### DRINK
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: C (as if holding a cup); palm left (in to the midline).
- Location: in front of the chin, moving to the mouth.
- Movement: "Move the hand to your mouth in a short arc". The hand tips back (the thumb side tilts toward the face) as if drinking.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/d/drink.htm

### HOME
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: O-flat ("fingers and thumb together").
- Location: cheek. First touch near the corner of the mouth, second touch "an inch or two toward your ear". Palm in/down.
- Movement: two touches moving toward the ear. The reverse order (ear to mouth) is also common.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/home.htm

### WORK
- Hands: two. L is a static base, R moves.
- Handshape: S on both hands, "palms facing downward".
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest/stomach. L fist is held horizontal.
- Movement: R fist taps the back of L's wrist "a few times" (usually 2), with the heel of the fist.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/work.htm

### TODAY
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: Y on both hands, "palms up".
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest/stomach.
- Movement: short drop "downward twice" (= double NOW). The older compound form NOW + DAY is still accepted.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/2939/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/t/today.htm

### TOMORROW
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: A-open ("10", thumb up).
- Start: "tip of thumb in contact with lower cheek, palm facing left" (toward the midline).
- Movement: "move forward at wrist in an arc motion". The thumb leaves the cheek and arcs forward, ending pointing forward/up. Single movement; a double movement = DAILY.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.handspeak.com/word/2233/ ; https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/t/tomorrow.htm

### WHAT
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: relaxed 5 (B-open/5 spread).
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest/waist, palms up, fingers forward.
- Movement: "The hands move slightly forward and to the sides" (a small shake outward).
- Non-manual: required wh-question face: brows furrowed, head jutted slightly forward, shoulders slightly raised.
- Note: the older variant (R index drawn down across L's open palm) is less common now.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/what.htm

### WHERE
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: 1 (index up).
- Location: neutral space at shoulder height, palm forward.
- Movement: "pivot the hand side to side a couple of times. The movement is in the wrist and elbow NOT in the finger joint."
- Non-manual: wh-question face (brows furrowed).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/where.htm

### WHO
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: L (thumb and index extended).
- Location: "tip of your thumb on your chin". Palm left/in, index points up/forward.
- Movement: "Bend your index finger twice" (index flexes, like L to bent L).
- Non-manual: wh-question face; lips often rounded ("oo").
- Variant: X-hand in front of the mouth with the index wiggling, no contact.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/who.htm

### HOW
- Hands: two.
- Handshape: curved hands (B-bent / C-like, thumbs alongside) on both.
- Start: knuckles (backs of the bent fingers) of both hands touch each other in front of the chest; "palms down and/or slightly back".
- Movement: "Roll the hands forward until the 'arches' are upside down--ending with your hands palm-up." The hands rotate out and forward around the contact point.
- Non-manual: wh-question face.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/how.htm

### WHY
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: starts B-open/bent fingers touching the forehead, changes to Y.
- Start: fingertips on the side of the forehead, palm in ("palm facing you").
- Movement: hand moves forward and down from the forehead to in front of the face/chin while changing to Y, palm still in.
- Non-manual: wh-question face.
- Variant: ends with the middle finger wiggling instead of Y.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/why.htm

### WANT
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: 5 changing to 5-claw.
- Start: neutral space in front of the chest/waist, "palms up", fingers forward.
- Movement: "Bring the hands back toward you and change them into 'claw' shapes." Single (or double) pull toward the body.
- Non-manual: may mouth "want"; brows can show desire.
- Note: DON'T-WANT turns the palms down after the pull.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/want.htm

### KNOW
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: B-bent / B (flat hand, fingers slightly bent).
- Location: "touches the side of your forehead with the tips of the fingers". Palm in/down, fingertips on the temple area. Casual signing often lowers it to the cheek.
- Movement: a single tap (double tap = FAMILIAR/AWARE).
- Non-manual: none (DON'T-KNOW: flick out, shoulder raise, head shake).
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/k/know.htm

### UNDERSTAND
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: S/X with the index fingertip held by the thumb pad (a "flick" position), changing to 1.
- Location: near the forehead/temple; palm in (back of the hand toward the viewer).
- Movement: "flick your index finger so that it points up". Single flick.
- Non-manual: brows up or a nod of realization.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/u/understand.htm

### MORE
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: O-flat on both ("flattened 'O' hands").
- Location: neutral space in front of the chest. Palms face each other (in toward the midline) / slightly down, fingertips toward each other.
- Movement: "Bring both 'O' hands together". Fingertips tap together, usually twice.
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/m/more.htm

### FINISH / DONE
- Hands: two, symmetric (one-handed is also used).
- Handshape: 5 (open, spread).
- Start: neutral space at chest height, "Each hand should face you, with your fingers pointing upward" (palms in).
- Movement: "Twist both hands quickly a couple times ending with the palms pointing (somewhat) forward." The forearms rotate outward. Single twist = emphatic.
- Non-manual: often mouths "fish" (lips) or "done".
- Confidence: **high**. https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/f/finish.htm

### WEATHER
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: W on both.
- Location: neutral space in front of the upper chest/shoulders, palms down/forward.
- Movement: "a wavy downward movement". The hands drop with a slight wiggle or flutter.
- Variant: W hands facing each other with small twists (like CHANGE).
- Non-manual: none.
- Confidence: **med** (handshape and movement from text; palm direction from the standard form). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/weather.htm

### RAIN
- Hands: two, symmetric.
- Handshape: 5-claw or 5 (fingers spread, slightly bent).
- Location: neutral space at head/shoulder height, palms down, fingers down/forward.
- Movement: hands move down twice with a slight bounce ("fingertips represent droplets"). A sideways slant = windy rain; a tense face = heavy rain.
- Non-manual: optional intensity face.
- Confidence: **med** (concept text only; handshape and movement are the standard citation form). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/r/rain.htm

### SUN
- Hands: one (R).
- Handshape: O-flat changing to 5 (or C at the start in the variant).
- Location: above the head / up and to the side, palm down/forward.
- Movement: "Circle a flattened 'O' handshape once and then bring it downward an inch or two as you open it up" (rays of light toward the face).
- Variant: C hand starts near the eye and moves up into the "sky".
- Non-manual: optionally squint.
- Confidence: **med** (several regional variants exist; the main version is quoted). https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/s/sun.htm
